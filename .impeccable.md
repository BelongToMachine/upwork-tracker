## Design Context

### Users
One person tracking Upwork job opportunities on their local computer. They need to scan many listings quickly, judge fit, and keep application follow-up state current.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, organized, practical. The interface should feel calm and dependable while keeping the work list easy to scan.

### Aesthetic Direction
Warm light workbench with a compact, table-first layout, warm paper-like surfaces, dark ink text, and restrained olive accents. Avoid dashboard-card clutter and decorative gradients.

### Design Principles
- Keep the job table as the visual center of the product.
- Make pay, competition, posted time, interest, and application state scannable at a glance.
- Use progressive disclosure for long descriptions and editing details.
- Keep local data entry and backup visible without crowding the main workflow.
- Use clear labels and keyboard-accessible controls with restrained motion.
